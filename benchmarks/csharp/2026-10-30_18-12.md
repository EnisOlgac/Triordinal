### Moving subtrees

A random tree is built, then 20,000 times a random node (not the root) is moved with its subtree to a random new parent outside that subtree. Since 2.3.0 a moved subtree is placed as one block; a block that fits into its gap is a fitted paste, otherwise a region is renumbered. Counts are for the moves only, per list (Q1 / Q2).
