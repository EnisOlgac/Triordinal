Rational against integer positions

The same Euler tour on one list, once with DTree's rational positions (DList: mediants, a region is renumbered when a denominator would exceed 2^40 or a numerator 2^61) and once with integer labels as in classical order maintenance (midpoints in [0, 2^62], a region is relabeled when no integer is left between two neighbors). Both renumber with the same density rule, so only the kind of position differs.
