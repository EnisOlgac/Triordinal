Kinds of positions

The same Euler tour on one list with four kinds of position: rational (DList: mediants, a region is renumbered when a denominator or numerator would exceed 2^61); integer, as in classical order maintenance (midpoints in [0, 2^62], a region is relabeled when no integer is left between two neighbors); double (midpoints); and decimal (the left neighbor plus half the gap rounded at its most significant digit, as in the original List.cs). Next to SOURCE or SINK, double and decimal step one unit outward, as rationals do. All renumber with the same density rule, so only the kind of position differs.

Placement rules

Where a new position goes between two neighbors: the midpoint, or the left neighbor plus half the gap rounded at its most significant digit in base 2 or 10 (MSD), the rule of the original List.cs. Same Euler tour and density rule as above; rational positions always take the mediant.

DTree and EulerTree

DTree keeps two lists, Q1 and Q2; EulerTree (Triordinal.DTree.Euler) keeps Q1 alone, with its closing cells, and decides reachability by whether a node lies inside another's block. Both with rational positions (the default) and with integer positions (Positions.Integer).
