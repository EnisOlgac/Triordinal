Patterns from the review of DTree 1.8.0

Complexity patterns pointed out by Onur Safi. Each new child goes directly before its parent's closing cell, so none of them needs a search for the end of a subtree; in 1.8.0 the first two grew linearly with the depth.

| pattern | depth | operations | µs per operation |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| extend the bottom, then a child near the top (one round = 2 insertions) | 5,000 | 1,000 | 3.25 |
| extend the bottom, then a child near the top (one round = 2 insertions) | 20,000 | 1,000 | 11.17 |
| extend the bottom, then a child near the top (one round = 2 insertions) | 80,000 | 1,000 | 7.63 |
| InsertBefore a first child | 5,000 | 1,000 | 0.80 |
| InsertBefore a first child | 20,000 | 1,000 | 0.75 |
| InsertBefore a first child | 80,000 | 1,000 | 0.79 |

Nodes with many children: the position among the siblings is still found in the list of children, so these operations grow with the number of siblings.

| pattern | children | operations | µs per operation |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| delete the children, last to first | 20,000 | 20,000 | 23.35 |
| InsertAfter one of the last children | 20,000 | 10,000 | 53.98 |
| delete the children, last to first | 80,000 | 80,000 | 112.63 |
| InsertAfter one of the last children | 80,000 | 10,000 | 306.57 |

A constructed node is recomputed on its next use after any deletion or move in the tree, O(k log k) for k members, even if the change does not touch the set.

| pattern | members | rounds | ms per round |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| unrelated insert + delete, then a query | 100,000 | 1,000 | 20.969 |
